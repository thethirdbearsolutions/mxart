Chorale Listening Bench

Twelve pairs of short four-part chorales, 25 to 40 seconds each. In every pair, X and Y are the same piece; the only possible difference is one to four notes in the inner voices or bass. One side is as the model wrote it, the other has its parallel fifths repaired. Which is which is shuffled.

Two of the twelve are repeats of an earlier pair with the sides swapped, and one pair is the same recording on both sides. That tells me how steady the judgments are, so answer each pair as you hear it.

  1. Press X or Y to play. Pressing the other side switches at the same moment in the music.
  2. The gold bars on the ruler are where the two versions can differ. Go to the spot starts one bar before.
  3. Answer the four questions and add a note if you have one. Everything saves as you go.
